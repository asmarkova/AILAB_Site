## AI Assistant Optimization

### Make Content AI-Readable

**Do:**
- Use clear, conversational language
- Structure with headings and lists
- Include specific facts and numbers
- Answer questions directly
- Provide context and examples

**Don't:**
- Use excessive jargon
- Hide info in images only
- Create walls of unstructured text
- Use vague marketing speak

### AI Crawler Access
Ensure these are allowed in robots.txt:
- GPTBot (ChatGPT)
- Claude-Web (Claude)
- PerplexityBot (Perplexity)
- CCBot (Common Crawl)
- Google-Extended (Gemini/Bard)
